Best results

Pick the right language and dialect. Hold the phone within 1–3 metres of the speaker with nothing covering the mic. Captions appear a second or two after each sentence. Very noisy places and whispers from far away will still be missed sometimes.

Free limit

Google's free plan allows about 500 caption requests a day, roughly an hour of steady talk. It resets at midnight US Pacific time (evening in Sydney). For unlimited use, turn on billing for your key in Google AI Studio; it costs around US$0.10 per hour of talk.

Privacy

Short audio clips go to Google to be heard and translated. On Google's free plan, Google may use them to improve its products. Your key and transcript stay on this phone.

Best accuracy

Pick the right language and dialect first; the phone listens for one language at a time. Keep the phone within 1–2 metres of the speaker. Whispering, music and crowd noise lower accuracy, and no speech engine is perfect.

If it can't hear Persian or Arabic

Update the Google app from the Play Store. Live Translate uses Google's speech service, which handles both languages.

Privacy

Google's speech service turns voice into text. The text goes to the translator you choose. Your API key and transcript stay on this phone.
